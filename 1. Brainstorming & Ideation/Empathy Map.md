# Empathy Map — PocketSmart AI User

> Persona: a budget-conscious Indian consumer planning a home purchase, an event, or a jewelry purchase.

## Says
- "I don't want to go over budget, but I also don't want to compromise too much."
- "I don't have time to compare five shopping apps for every item."
- "Will this actually look good with what I'm wearing / where I'm hosting?"
- "I just want a plan I can act on right now."

## Thinks
- "What's a *reasonable* price for this in India, not the US?"
- "How should I split this budget across categories?"
- "Can I trust an AI's product suggestions, or will it hallucinate prices?"
- "I'll probably want to revisit this plan later — I shouldn't have to redo the work."

## Does
- Opens multiple shopping tabs (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra...) and compares manually.
- Uses a notes app or spreadsheet to track a rough budget split.
- Asks friends/family for recommendations on vendors or products.
- Abandons the plan midway because it takes too long to finish properly.

## Feels
- Overwhelmed by choice and scattered information.
- Anxious about overspending or missing a category (e.g. no contingency fund).
- Hopeful that an AI tool could shortcut the research step.
- Frustrated when a tool gives generic, non-India-relevant, or unlinked suggestions.

## Pains
- Budget math (percentage allocation, remaining budget) done manually and error-prone.
- No single tool understands both "budget constraint" and "Indian shopping platforms" together.
- Jewelry/outfit matching is purely visual and hard to search for in text.
- No memory of past plans — every session starts from zero.

## Gains
- A single structured plan: category breakdown, item-level suggestions, and remaining budget.
- Direct, clickable search links to real Indian platforms — no manual searching.
- Multimodal matching (outfit photo → jewelry suggestions) that feels personalized.
- A saved history to revisit and compare past plans.
