# Define Problem Statements

> This document supersedes the placeholder PDF in this folder (kept for reference) with the actual problem statements for **PocketSmart AI**.

## PS-1 — Home Interior Shoppers

**I am** a first-time homeowner or renter furnishing a new space.
**I'm trying to** buy lighting, fans, and furniture that fit my style and my rooms.
**But** I don't know how to split a fixed budget across categories, or which products at which price points are actually good value.
**Because** product research across many shopping sites takes hours and I have no frame of reference for "reasonable" prices in India.
**Which makes me feel** overwhelmed and worried I'll overspend on the wrong things or underspend and end up with a half-furnished home.

## PS-2 — Event/Party Organizers

**I am** someone planning a birthday, wedding, or corporate event on a fixed budget.
**I'm trying to** book a venue, caterer, decorator, and entertainment that together fit inside that budget.
**But** I have no easy way to see how much of the budget each category should reasonably take, or who offers that in my area.
**Because** vendor pricing is scattered across many apps (Swiggy, Zomato, BookMyShow, OYO, etc.) with no single place to plan proportionally.
**Which makes me feel** anxious about going over budget or forgetting a category altogether (e.g., no contingency for last-minute costs).

## PS-3 — Jewelry Buyers Matching an Outfit

**I am** someone attending an occasion (wedding, festival, party) who wants jewelry that matches a specific outfit.
**I'm trying to** find jewelry that complements the outfit's color and formality, within a set budget.
**But** I can't easily describe "what goes with this" in a search bar, and I don't want to overspend chasing the "perfect" piece.
**Because** jewelry recommendations today are generic catalogs, not matched to an actual photo of what I'm wearing.
**Which makes me feel** unsure of my choices and likely to either overspend for safety or settle for a mismatched look.

## PS-4 — Budget-Conscious Decision Makers in General

**I am** a budget-conscious consumer across any of the above scenarios.
**I'm trying to** make confident purchase decisions quickly.
**But** I have to manually do currency-aware price research, percentage math, and vendor comparison every time.
**Because** no single tool combines AI reasoning about a budget with real, clickable Indian shopping links.
**Which makes me feel** like the process is more work than the decision itself deserves.
