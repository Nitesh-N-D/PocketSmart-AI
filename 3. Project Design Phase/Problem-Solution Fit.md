# Problem-Solution Fit Canvas

## Customer Segment (CS)
Budget-conscious consumers in India making a discrete, high-decision-cost purchase: furnishing a home, planning an event, or buying jewelry for an occasion.

## Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)
- Turn a single number (the budget) into an actionable, itemized plan.
- Find real, purchasable products/services that fit within that plan.
- Do this quickly, without manually researching across many sites.

## Triggers
- Moving into a new home.
- An upcoming birthday, wedding, or corporate event.
- An upcoming occasion requiring an outfit + matching jewelry.

## Emotions (before / after)
- **Before:** overwhelmed, time-pressured, afraid of overspending.
- **After:** confident in a concrete plan, able to act immediately via shopping links.

## Available Solutions (existing alternatives)
- Manual spreadsheets + browser tabs across Amazon, Flipkart, Swiggy, etc.
- Generic budgeting apps (track spend, don't recommend products).
- In-store/vendor consultations (slow, not budget-structured).
- Generic AI chatbots (no structured JSON, no shopping links, no history).

## Channels of Behaviour
- Search engines for "budget furniture ideas", "party planning budget India".
- Shopping apps directly, comparing manually.
- Social media / influencer recommendations.

## PocketSmart AI's Fit
PocketSmart AI directly targets the gap between "I have a budget and a vague idea" and "I have an itemized, India-priced, shoppable plan" — using Gemini to do the category allocation and product reasoning that today is done manually, and attaching real search links so the plan is immediately actionable.
